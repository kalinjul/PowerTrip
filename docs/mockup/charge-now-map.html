Phone UI · issue #192

Charge now on the map

While the "Jetzt laden" sheet is open, the map behind it shows the same candidates as the list. Today the modal sheet dims the map and the map keeps showing every charger in the viewport, so the driver can't tell which three are meant. Three ways to fix that, all with the same sheet.

TodayModal sheet, map unchanged

For reference: the scrim dims the map, the candidates are among a dozen other pills.

ANumbered discs

The top three become numbered discs in the colour of the card's rank badge. The other chargers stay, faded. Cheapest: one new marker, the pills stay as they are.

BRanked pills, rest hidden

Candidates keep their normal pill (speed, network, free count) with the rank in front and a ring in the badge colour. Every other charger is hidden; "Mehr in der Nähe" shows as small dots.

CLinked selection

Like A, but list and map share a selection: the selected candidate grows into a callout with name and distance, its card gets a ring. Tapping a disc selects its card; tapping a card selects its disc.

The same in every variant

  • No scrim. The sheet stays modal for back and swipe-down, but the map above it is not dimmed, otherwise there is nothing to see.
  • The camera fits the driver's position and the top three into the part of the map above the sheet, once, when the results arrive. Afterwards the driver may pan.
  • Closing the sheet brings back the normal map: all chargers, and the camera where the driver had it before.
  • Fully expanded sheet covers the map; nothing to show then, the markers simply stay.
  • Loading, empty, no position: the map shows no candidates and keeps its normal markers.

Open questions

  • Hide or fade the other chargers? Hiding (B) tells the clearest story; fading (A, C) keeps the context, e.g. a full site next to a candidate.
  • Show "Mehr in der Nähe" on the map? Only B does, as dots. Up to a dozen more markers dilute the top three.
  • Restore the old camera on close, or stay where the candidates are?
