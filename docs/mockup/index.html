PowerTrip

Plan the charge. Let Google Maps drive.

EV route planning across Europe: charging stops picked for your car, priced per network and tariff, with live traffic — and navigation handed straight to Google Maps.

Demo script — everything is tappable
  • 1Charge now — the three best free chargers nearby, ranked by your filters. If the filters starve the list, they're relaxed automatically: min power → networks → max price → max distance.
  • 2Plan — Amsterdam → München with live traffic and priced stops.
  • 3Send the whole route, a marked section, or a single stop to Google Maps.
  • 4☰ menu — car, subscriptions, and a searchable networks view. Exclude Ionity, watch everything re-plan.
  • 5♡ — saved and recent routes; favorite a recent one, or save a planned route right from the route view's heart button.

    • All data is fake, every button still does something. Disposable HTML mockup — the real app will be Kotlin Multiplatform with live data.
